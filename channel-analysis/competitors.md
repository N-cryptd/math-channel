## Analysis Notes

### Patterns Across Top Performers
1. **Title optimization**: Top channels use curiosity gaps ("But what IS...", "Why X is Y", "How X actually works")
2. **Video length**: 10-20 minutes is the sweet spot for math education
3. **Color coding**: Consistent color associations (blue = one concept, red = another) across series
4. **Intuition before rigor**: All top channels build visual/geometric intuition before formal definitions
5. **Curriculum structure**: Most successful channels organize videos into named series/playlists
6. **Thumbnail patterns**: High-contrast text overlay on dark background, single key image/animation frame
7. **Pacing**: Start with a hook/motivation, build up complexity, end with a satisfying resolution
8. **Audio quality**: Clear narration is as important as visuals — professional voiceover
