## Competitive Analysis Summary

**Market gap:** No major animation channel covers metrization theorems with Manim. Metric spaces are usually taught in real analysis, not topology. The Urysohn Metrization Theorem and Nagata-Smirnov theorem are pure topology results that bridge analysis and topology — perfect for visual treatment.

**Our opportunity:** Visual treatment of:
- Distance functions and how they induce topologies
- Which topological spaces come from metric spaces (metrization)
- Urysohn's Metrization Theorem: regular + second countable + T1 => metrizable
- Examples of non-metrizable topologies (cofinite topology)
