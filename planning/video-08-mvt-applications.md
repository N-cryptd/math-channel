# Video 08: Mean Value Theorem & Applications
## Overview
- **Topic**: The Mean Value Theorem, Rolle's Theorem, increasing/decreasing functions, and the First Derivative Test
- **Hook**: "If you drive 60 miles in 1 hour, at some instant your speed was exactly 60 mph."
- **Aha moment**: The MVT is the bridge between average and instantaneous rates — and it's the most important theoretical tool in calculus
- **Target audience**: Watched Videos 01-07
- **Length**: ~12 min

## Scenes
1. **Hook** — The speeding ticket scenario: average speed 60 mph means at some moment your speed WAS 60 mph. The Mean Value Theorem says if f is continuous on [a,b] and differentiable on (a,b), there exists c such that f'(c) = (f(b)-f(a))/(b-a). (~60s)
2. **Visual** — Draw a curve with secant line through (a,f(a)) and (b,f(b)). Show there's a tangent parallel to the secant somewhere in between. Color the tangent in ACCENT. (~90s)
3. **Rolle's Theorem** — Special case where f(a)=f(b). Then f'(c)=0 somewhere. Show visually: curve that starts and ends at same height has a horizontal tangent. (~45s)
4. **Proof intuition** — Subtract the secant line, apply Rolle's Theorem to the difference function. Show the algebra. (~60s)
5. **Increasing/Decreasing** — If f'(x) > 0 on an interval, f is increasing. If f'(x) < 0, f is decreasing. Show with colored curve segments. Identify critical points where f'(x)=0. (~60s)
6. **First Derivative Test** — At a critical point: if f' changes + to -, local max. If - to +, local min. If no change, neither. Animated sign chart. (~60s)
7. **Recap** — "Next: Curve sketching — concavity and the Second Derivative Test." (~45s)
