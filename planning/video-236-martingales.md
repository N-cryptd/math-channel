# Video 236: Martingales
Stochastic Processes playlist, video 8/12. Est. duration: 15 min.

## Topic
Martingales: definition, examples (random walk, product of i.i.d. mean-1 rvs),
submartingales and supermartingales, martingale transforms.

## Prerequisites
- Video 230: Markov Chains
- Video 235: Brownian Motion

## Scenes
### Scene 1: Hook — fair games, no profit strategy
### Scene 2: Definition — E[X_{n+1} | F_n] = X_n
### Scene 3: Examples — symmetric RW, likelihood ratios
### Scene 4: Sub/Supermartingales
### Scene 5: Martingale Transform (predictable processes)
### Scene 6: Summary

## Key Formulas
- E[X_{n+1} | F_n] = X_n
- Sub: E[X_{n+1} | F_n] >= X_n
- Super: E[X_{n+1} | F_n] <= X_n
