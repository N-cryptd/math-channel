# Video 90: Why Proofs? (Introduction to Proof-Based Mathematics)

## Overview
- **Video Number:** 90
- **Playlist:** Introduction to Proofs (L4 — Proof-Based Mathematics)
- **Estimated Duration:** 12 min
- ** OR 5-7 min if we want a gentler intro** — target 10 min
- **Prerequisites:** Discrete Mathematics basics (logic, sets)

## Learning Objectives
1. Understand what a mathematical proof is and why it matters
2. Learn the distinction between intuition / plausibility and rigorous proof
3. See the anatomy of a simple direct proof
4. Preview the proof techniques we'll cover in this playlist

## Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1: Hook (0:00–1:00)
- **Content:** Ask the viewer: "Why do we need proofs? Can't we just check a bunch of examples?" Show why examples are dangerous.
- **Visual:** Number line with scattered checked points, then a counterexample appearing far away.
- **Budget:** Title + 2 mobjects (number line, counterexample dot) = 3 items

### Scene 2: The Need for Proof (1:00–3:30)
- **Content:** Conjecture: "Every number of the form n² + n + 41 deputies is prime" (Euler's polynomial). Works for n = 0 to 39, fails at n = 40.
- **Visual:** Grid of 40 green checkmarks, then one big red X at n = 40.

- **Budget:** Formula + checkmarks (progressive) + red X = 3 items max

### Scene 3: What IS a Proof? (3:30–5:30)
- **Content:** Definition — a proof is a sequence of logical deductions from axioms to a conclusion.
- **Visual:** Chain of labeled blocks: AXIOMS → DEDUCTIONS → THEOREM
- **Budget:** 3 blocks + arrows = 3 items

### Scene 4: Types of Proofs Preview (5:30–8:00)
- **Content:** Introduce direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction, induction — with one-line examples showing the logical structure.
- **Visual:** Four small cards with icons, progressive reveal one by one.
- **Budget per reveal:** 1 card = 1 item

### Scene 5: A First Direct Proof (8:00–10:30)
- **Content:** Prove: "The sum of two even integers is even."
- **Visual:** Statement → let a = 2k, b = 2m → algebra → conclusion. Step-by-step reveal.
- **Budget:** Statement + one line of work = 2 items

### Scene 6: Recap + Outro (10:30–12:00)
- **Content:** Summary + preview of next video (Direct Proof)
- **Visual:** Bullet recap + "Next: Direct Proof"
- **Budget:** recap list + next card = 2 items

## Key Formulas
- Euler's: n² + n + 41, n = 40 → 1681 = 41²
- Even number: a = 2k, b = 2m → a + b = 2(k + m)

## Narration Notes
- Keep tone conversational but precise
- Emphasize the shock of the n = 40 counterexample — it's emotionally memorable
- Connect proofs to "knowing WHY something is true, not just THAT it's true"
