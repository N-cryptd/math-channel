### Scene 2: Groups (70s)
**Content budget:** title + 4 items
**Narration (~35s):** "Videos 217 and 218 covered advanced group theory. Group actions formalize symmetry. Solvable groups are those with a terminating derived series. Nilpotent groups have a stronger property via the lower central series. The Sylow theorems constrain the structure of finite groups. These tools are the algebraic foundation for everything that follows."

- Title: "Part I: Groups"
- Items: group actions, solvable groups, nilpotent groups, Sylow theorems

### Scene 3: Fields and Extensions (70s)
**Content budget:** title + 4 items
**Narration (~35s):** "Videos 219 through 221 built field theory. Field extensions measure how much bigger one field is than another. Algebraic extensions are the kind where every element satisfies a polynomial. Splitting fields contain all roots of a given polynomial, and their degree is bounded by n factorial. These are the stage on which Galois theory plays."

- Title: "Part II: Fields and Extensions"
- Items: field extensions, algebraic extensions, splitting fields, degree

### Scene 4: Galois Theory (80s)
**Content budget:** title + 5 items
**Narration (~40s):** "Videos 222 through 225 are the heart of the playlist. Galois groups capture the symmetries of field extensions. The Fundamental Theorem of Galois Theory establishes a perfect correspondence between subfields and subgroups. Solvability by radicals reduces to solvability of the Galois group. And the Abel-Ruffini theorem, the climax, shows the general quintic is unsolvable because S5 is not a solvable group. This is one of the greatest achievements in mathematics."

- Title: "Part III: Galois Theory (The Heart)"
- Items: Galois groups, FTGT, solvability by radicals, Abel-Ruffini, A5 simplicity

### Scene 5: Special Topics (70s)
**Content budget:** title + 3 items
**Narration (~35s):** "Videos 226 and 227 explored two beautiful families of fields. Cyclotomic fields, generated by roots of unity, have abelian Galois groups and connect to constructibility and number theory. Finite fields, with prime power order, have cyclic Galois groups generated by the Frobenius automorphism, and power modern applications in coding and cryptography."

- Title: "Part IV: Cyclotomic and Finite Fields"
- Items: cyclotomic fields + (Z/nZ)*, finite fields + Frobenius, applications

### Scene 6: The Grand Connection (60s)
**Content budget:** title + central diagram
**Narration (~30s):** "The grand connection: solving polynomial equations by radicals is equivalent to computing whether the Galois group is solvable. Groups control fields through the FTGT. Field extensions encode polynomial roots. And polynomials encode the equations we want to solve. This triangle of groups, fields, and polynomials is Galois' immortal insight."

- Title: "The Grand Connection"
- Triangle: Groups <-> Fields <-> Polynomials (all connected)
