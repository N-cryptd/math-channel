### Scene 5: The Unifying Theme — Unitary Operators (100s)
- Everything connects through unitary operators on Hilbert spaces
- Fourier series: unitary on L²[-π,π]
- Fourier transform: unitary on L²(R)
- Unitarity explains: Parseval, Plancherel, F⁴ = I
- "Fourier analysis is the study of change of orthonormal basis"
