# Full Mathematics Curriculum Map

## Legend
- **L1** = Beginner (no prerequisites)
- **L2** = Intermediate (high school math)
- **L3** = Advanced (calculus required)
- **L4** = Undergraduate (proof-based math)
- **L5** = Graduate (advanced undergraduate required)

## Recommended Launch Order
1. Calculus I (highest demand, builds audience)
2. Linear Algebra (second highest demand)
3. Pre-Calculus (feeds into Calculus)
4. Calculus II (natural continuation)
5. Real Analysis I (first proof-based course)
6. Calculus III (multivariable)
7. ODEs (applied calculus)
8. Abstract Algebra I
9. Complex Analysis
10. Probability & Statistics
