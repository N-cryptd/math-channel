## Topics
1. Opening hook: how do we measure the "size" of a vector or function?
2. The hierarchy of spaces: vector -> inner product -> normed -> metric -> topological
3. Formal definition of a norm and its three axioms
4. Geometric intuition: unit balls in R^2 for different norms (L1, L2, L-infinity)
5. How the unit ball shape changes as p varies (animated morphing)
6. Examples of normed spaces: R^n with standard norms, sequence spaces (l^p, l^infinity), function spaces (C[a,b], L^p)
7. From norm to metric: every norm induces a metric d(x,y) = ||x - y||
8. Properties of norms: homogeneity, triangle inequality, consequences
9. Equivalence of norms on finite-dimensional spaces (visual)
10. Convergence in normed spaces: what does ||x_n - x|| -> 0 mean?
11. Teaser: not every normed space is complete -- that's Banach spaces (next video)
12. Summary + what's next
